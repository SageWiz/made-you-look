How it works

The photo gets cut into a grid of cells. For each cell, the page averages how dark the pixels inside it are and picks a character with about that much ink: a space for the lightest patches, an @ for the darkest. It happens in your browser, which is why the slider can redraw it on the spot.

Dragging doesn't redraw anything. The characters are a layer sitting on top of the photo, and the handle decides how much of that layer you can see. Both layers are the same size, so every character sits exactly over the patch of photo it stands for.
