Search box that builds the page

The page starts as one box. What you ask decides which parts of it appear.

Or try:

Nothing here yet. The page shows up when you ask for it.

How it works

There's no AI behind this. The page carries a short list of facts about me, and each group of facts is tagged with words people might use to ask for it. Your question gets split into words, the words get scored against those tags, and the best-scoring groups are added to the page as you type.

If nothing scores high enough, it assumes the question isn't about me and points you to Google. Nothing you type leaves your browser unless you take that link.
