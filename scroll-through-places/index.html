Scroll through places

Scrolling pushes you into one place until the next one opens up inside it. No clicking, no slides, just the scroll you were already doing.

Three stops, starting right below. Skip to the write-up

Stand-in rooms. Each one gets swapped for a photo of a place I've worked or shot in.

How it works

Every place is a full-screen layer. The one you're in scales up as you scroll, and the next one sits in the middle of it at 30% size, growing at the same rate. When the small one reaches full size it becomes the one you're in. One number ties those scales together, so there's no jump at the handoff.

It runs on the browser's own scrolling. The section is very tall and the view sticks to the top of the window while you pass through. Your scrollbar, keyboard, trackpad and thumb all work the way they always do.
