01 First build is up

Head-tracked window

The screen acts like a pane of glass. Move your head and the room behind it shifts the way a real one would.

Steer with your head?

Your browser will ask for the camera next. The video is read in this tab to find your face. It isn't uploaded, saved, or shown to anyone.

Placeholder room. The real one will be a 3D capture of somewhere I've actually stood.

How it works

A face-tracking model runs in the browser and reports where your eyes are about thirty times a second. That position gets treated as the spot you're looking from, and the view is skewed to match it. Lean left and you see more of the right wall, the same as leaning at a real window.

Without a camera, the mouse or the tilt of your phone supplies the position. It's the same effect with a less magic input.
