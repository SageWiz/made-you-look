Made You Look

A deep dive into the UX and UI that grabs people's attention. I keep saving websites that made me stop scrolling, then I rebuild the interesting part and write down what it would cost to put in front of real users.

Every entry ends on the same question: would I ship it?
01 First build is up

Head-tracked window

The screen acts like a pane of glass. Your webcam follows your head, and the room behind the glass shifts the way a real one would. Say no to the camera and your mouse does the same job.

Seen in a reel by Dominik Fojcik, who did it with a 3D capture of a real corridor.
